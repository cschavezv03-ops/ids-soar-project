Baseline threshold, chosen on TRAINING data only:
    rule:            flow_pkts_s <= 0.203715 -> ATTACK
    how:             swept 400 distinct candidate
                     thresholds, taken as quantiles of the training
                     values (observed values, never interpolated),
                     in BOTH directions, keeping the best attack F1
    its train F1:    0.6049

    Both directions, on training data:
        flow_pkts_s >=     0.077608   F1 = 0.2858
        flow_pkts_s <=     0.203715   F1 = 0.6049  <- kept
        (a classifier that flags EVERY flow scores 0.2857)

    The operational rule is worded 'more than N connections in 10
    seconds', i.e. HIGH rate = attack. On this data that direction is
    worth essentially nothing: it scores what a constant 'everything
    is an attack' classifier scores. Median flow_pkts_s is 0.19 for
    attacks and 54.42 for benign traffic - the dataset's attacks are
    the SLOW flows, because a DoS holds connections open and a scan
    probe is one packet in a flow that then waits. The direction is
    therefore fitted on TRAINING data like the threshold, so that the
    comparison in A5 is against the rule at its best and not against
    a strawman of our own wording.
