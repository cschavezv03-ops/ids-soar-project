3. THE DECISION
==============================================================================
    contain  0.70 -> 0.50
    HIGH     0.90 -> 0.70

WHAT IT BUYS, on lab traffic:
    port scan detection   0.6% -> 57.1%
    benign false positives 59 -> 193 of 12,029

WHAT IT COSTS, on CICIDS2017 - and it costs almost nothing, because
recall rises on every single family while precision drops by 0.0019:

HIGH moves from 0.90 to 0.70 because nothing in the lab reaches 0.90 -
the highest probability any lab attack flow receives is 0.72. Left at 0.90 the
severity band would be dead on our own network, and a graded response
that never grades is not a design, it is decoration.

==============================================================================
4. WHAT NO THRESHOLD FIXES: THE SYN FLOOD
==============================================================================
The three flood captures - 10, 100 and 1000 packets per second -
produce 107,981 flows and 1 distinct feature vector(s) between them,
and every one of them scores 0.12. The intensity does not matter at all.

That answers the question the three intensities were captured to
answer: it is NOT a matter of speed.

The reason is structural. Our flood and CICIDS2017's DDoS are not the
same attack:

    feature                  lab hping3 -S   CICIDS2017 DDoS
    flow_duration                     0.00      1,882,981.00
    tot_fwd_pkts                      1.00              4.00
    tot_bwd_pkts                      0.00              4.00
    totlen_bwd_pkts                   0.00         11,601.00
    bwd_pkt_len_mean                  0.00          1,934.50
    pkt_len_std                       0.00          1,903.96

Ours is an UNANSWERED SYN flood: one packet out, nothing back, zero
duration. Theirs is an ANSWERED HTTP flood: four packets each way and
11,601 bytes of server response. They share a name and nothing else.

And the decisive number: CICIDS2017 contains 0 flows with our
flood's signature (1 forward packet, 0 backward). Not few - NONE.

So the model is not misclassifying the flood. It is EXTRAPOLATING into
a region of the feature space its training set never covered, and a
threshold cannot repair a region that was never learned.
