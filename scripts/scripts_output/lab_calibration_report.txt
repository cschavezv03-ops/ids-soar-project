6. CAPTURE QUALITY: PACKETS LARGER THAN THE MTU
==============================================================================
A packet longer than 1500 bytes did not travel that way. It is the
NIC coalescing several segments before the capture sees them (GRO/LRO/
TSO). It matters here more than usual: the three features the forest
leans on hardest are bwd_pkt_len_mean, _std and _max, and coalescing
inflates all three. CICIDS2017 carries no such artefact, so this alone
moves our traffic away from what the model learned.

    capture                              fwd max   bwd max   flows > 1500 B
    benigno_ssh.pcap                       1,672     1,580              1/1
    benigno_web.pcap                          77     7,240              1/1
    portscan_lento_corrected.pcap              6         6            0/118
    portscan_rapido_corrected.pcap             6         6           0/1147
    syn_flood_corrected.pcap                   6         2            0/100
    ssh_bruteforce.pcap                      976     1,048              0/3

Fix at capture time, not in the extractor - the extractor must keep
measuring what is on the wire:
    sudo ethtool -K <iface> gro off lro off tso off gso off

7. THE HALF THAT CANNOT BE MEASURED YET
==============================================================================
Benign flows available in the lab captures: 2

A6 measured a false positive rate of 0.00189 on CICIDS2017 benign
traffic. To confirm or refute that number on OUR network we need enough
benign flows for the estimate to have a usable interval:

      benign flows   expected FP        95% interval on the rate
                 2           0.0      [0.00022 , 0.66742]  <- what we have
             1,000           1.9      [0.00021 , 0.00537]
             5,000           9.4      [0.00088 , 0.00327]
            10,000          18.9      [0.00114 , 0.00283]
            50,000          94.5      [0.00153 , 0.00229]

With what we have the interval spans from a fraction of a percent to
most of the traffic: it cannot tell a working detector apart from one
that blocks two users in three. This is not a detail to note in passing
- it is the number the whole containment decision rests on.

WHY MORE SSH SESSIONS WILL NOT FIX IT. A2 section 5 already flagged it:
the benign captures are single long sessions, so one SSH login is ONE
flow, not hundreds. Reaching thousands of benign flows needs sustained,
varied traffic from several hosts - browsing, downloads, DNS - which is
also the FAST benign profile that A5 section 6.3 identified as the
riskiest, because CICIDS2017 associates speed with normal traffic.
